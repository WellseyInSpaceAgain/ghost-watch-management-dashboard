# Ghost Watch Economic Plan — v1

## Purpose

Build a small but compounding EVE Online economic engine that can eventually fund:

- Ghost Watch losses and replacement packages
- solo PvP losses
- future Tech III Strategic Cruiser production
- a PLEX/Omega reserve

The mature operating target is roughly:

- **5b ISK/month** as a strong operating target
- **10b ISK/month** as a stretch target

v1 is not about immediately reaching those numbers. It is about proving repeatable systems that can scale.

## Economic Structure

The v1 economy has two main industrial tracks, supported by trading and active ISK generation.

### Track A: T2 Workshop

Purpose:

> Generate cashflow while developing a repeatable industrial process and learning how to measure production properly.

Start with small test batches from blueprints already available to us, especially:

- Medium Shield Extender II
- Damage Control II
- Warp Scrambler II

Each product test should record:

- total input cost
- job cost
- expected sale value after fees
- realised revenue
- realised profit
- manufacturing time
- time to sell
- profit per slot-day
- capital tied up
- verdict: **Scale / Retest / Drop**

The important comparison is not percentage margin alone.

A useful comparison metric is:

> **ISK profit per slot-day per ISK of capital tied up**

A product with a large percentage margin but poor turnover may be worse than a product with a smaller margin that sells quickly and repeatedly.

As an initial preference, look for roughly **10%+ net margin**, useful absolute ISK profit and sensible turnover, but do not treat this as a rigid rule.

## Track B: Project Tengu

Project Tengu is the strategic research and development programme.

The North Star is:

> **Manufacture our own complete Ghost Watch T3Cs.**

The first target is a complete Ghost Watch Tengu:

- Tengu hull
- four required subsystems

Project Tengu starts immediately rather than waiting for the T2 Workshop to become mature.

The distinction is:

> **T2 is the commercial business. T3C is the strategic programme.**

The first self-built Tengu is allowed to be an **R&D expense**. It does not need to be profitable.

### Tengu Progression

**Hull #1**

Prove the pipeline end-to-end. Purchase intermediate materials or components where necessary.

**Hull #2**

Internalise more of the invention / reverse-engineering process.

**Hull #3**

Begin producing more of the T3 components internally.

**Later**

Consider polymer reactions and deeper vertical integration only where the economics or strategic value justify them.

### T3C Production Chain

Conceptually:

```text
Fullerites
    ↓
Hybrid Polymer Reactions
    ↓
Hybrid Polymers

Ancient Salvage
    +
Hybrid Polymers
    ↓
T3 Components

Relics
    +
Datacores
    ↓
T3 Hull / Subsystem BPCs

BPCs
    +
Components
    ↓
Tengu Hull + Subsystems
```

Purchased intermediates are completely acceptable during the learning phase.

The objective is to learn the chain, prove the process and progressively internalise useful stages rather than requiring complete vertical integration from day one.

- Material Efficiency / Time Efficiency research

His eventual direction should be determined by the first real bottleneck:

- **Factory Ray** if manufacturing slots become the constraint

## Capital Policy

Because the economic engine is still capital-poor, experimental programmes should not consume the entire bankroll.

Initial rough guardrails:

| Use | Initial Scale |
| --- | ---: |
| Core / Trading Capital | ~500–600m |
| T2 Workshop | ~100–150m |
| T3 R&D | ~50–75m initially |
| Reserve | Remaining capital |
| Ghost Watch Treasury | Primarily funded from realised profit |

These are guardrails, not permanent rules.

### Early Profit Allocation

A useful early-stage allocation is approximately:

```text
70% → Core Capital
20% → Ghost Watch Treasury
10% → T3 R&D
```

This is a growth-stage policy and can change as the economy matures.

### Capital Principle

> **Ghost Watch funding should come from profits rather than consuming the economic engine's principal.**

Core Capital should continue growing.

The Ghost Watch Treasury is the pool that tells us how much operational violence the economy can sustainably fund.

A mature operational metric is:

> **How many Ghost Watch replacement packages can currently be funded without touching Core Capital?**

## Second Account / Omega Expansion Gate

Do not reactivate the second account simply because Tradesey and Von have useful trained skills.

The second account should become Omega when several conditions are satisfied:

- Core Capital is around **2.5b ISK**
- trailing 30-day economic profit comfortably covers the cost of 500 PLEX
- there is demonstrated work or backlog for Tradesey or Von
- the extra account removes a genuine bottleneck
- expected incremental monthly value is roughly **1.5× the Omega cost or better**
- Ghost Watch funding remains healthy

Omega should be treated as a business expansion decision rather than an account that subsequently needs work invented for it.

## Planetary Interaction

Planetary Interaction should deliberately remain low-maintenance.

Wellsey already has strong relevant skills.

An initial approach is approximately:

- three colonies
- long extraction cycles
- infrequent interaction
- output that either feeds our own production or is easy to liquidate

Do not turn Planetary Interaction into another full-time activity or a multibox management exercise.

## Trading

Jita station trading remains part of the Core Capital engine.

Current Wellsey fee assumptions:

- buy broker fee: **1.3%**
- sell broker fee: **1.3%**
- sales tax: **4.2%**

Initial trading capital is around **600m ISK**.

Initial niches include:

- boosters
- implants
- another tightly defined niche rather than random market-wide trading

Trading should help generate capital while industrial systems become increasingly repeatable and scalable.

## Active ISK Injection

Industry and trading do not need to fund the entire economy immediately.

Fresh capital can still be injected through activities that are enjoyable, such as:

- Level 4 missions
- burner missions
- exploration
- C3 wormhole PvE
- combat exploration
- other active PvE where worthwhile

The useful distinction is:

> **Active PvE injects ISK. The economic system should increasingly multiply it.**

---

## Accounting Rule

> **Self-produced, self-mined or already-owned materials are not free.**

Inputs should be valued at realistic replacement or opportunity cost.

Otherwise a production run can appear profitable merely because valuable inputs were assigned a fictional cost of zero.

1. Keep Wellsey's trading book running.
2. Select a small number of existing T2 blueprint copies for testing.
3. Check current profitability before committing significant manufacturing capital.
4. Run small T2 production batches.
5. Record actual results and develop the T2 production playbook.
6. Give Ironman ownership of the research and invention workflow.
7. Start Project Tengu immediately.
8. Produce the first Tengu's complete **Bill Of Materials (BOM)**, science requirements, required facilities, production stages and current capability gaps.
9. Purchase intermediates for stages that cannot yet be performed internally.
10. Leave Ray's unallocated skill points untouched until T2 Workshop or Project Tengu exposes a genuine bottleneck.
11. Grow Core Capital and begin funding the Ghost Watch Treasury from realised profit.
12. Consider second-account Omega only after the expansion gate is genuinely satisfied.

## v1 Economic Model in One Paragraph

Trading and active PvE provide new capital. The T2 Workshop teaches us how to convert capital into repeatable commercial profit. Project Tengu develops the industrial knowledge and capability required to turn that economic engine into strategic Ghost Watch supply. Realised profit grows Core Capital while progressively funding the Ghost Watch Treasury and further research. Characters, skill points and subscriptions are expanded only when actual bottlenecks demonstrate the need.
